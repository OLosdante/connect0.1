Ideas for your next career move.

Practical advice for finding work, growing your skills, and making your next opportunity count.

Job search November 10, 2026

Make your next job search more focused

A clear plan can make looking for work feel less overwhelming. Start with the roles and workplaces that fit you best.

Read article

Begin by listing the skills you use confidently and the kind of work you want to do next. Use those priorities to narrow your search instead of applying to every opening you see.

Set aside regular time to find suitable roles, tailor your application to each one, and keep track of where and when you applied. A simple routine helps you stay organized and follow up thoughtfully.

Applications October 28, 2026

Write a résumé that is easy to understand

Help employers quickly see what you can do by keeping your résumé clear, relevant, and focused on results.

Read article

Put your most relevant experience and skills where they are easy to find. Use clear headings, concise descriptions, and consistent formatting so readers can scan the page quickly.

Whenever possible, describe the outcome of your work rather than only listing duties. Review the job description and highlight experience that genuinely matches its requirements. Keep all details accurate and proofread before sending.

Interviews October 15, 2026

Prepare for an interview with confidence

Thoughtful preparation helps you explain your experience and decide whether a role is right for you.

Read article

Learn about the organization and review the responsibilities in the job posting. Think of a few examples that show how you solve problems, work with others, and learn from challenges.

Prepare questions about the role, team, and expectations. During the conversation, take a moment to think before answering, and be honest about your experience. An interview is also your chance to understand the opportunity.
